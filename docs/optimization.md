## Method

The objective is the expected ticks from segment start to goal: the mean over seeds. Fast Downward
(`astar(lmcut())`) is the only search. Each action's cost is its measured mean ticks (rounded, at
least 1), with walks costed per link. This static cost table is a *surrogate*: real durations depend
on context, so the winner is chosen by measurement and reported on held-out seeds.

- Explore seeds 1-5: the optimistic loop. Unmeasured actions cost 1 (a lower bound); each plan with unmeasured actions is measured and its samples pooled, until the plan has only measured actions and repeats.
- Candidates: the mean-cost plan, the unit-cost plan and 20 plans from bootstrap-sampled cost vectors (RNG seed 0), deduplicated by action sequence.
- Select seeds 1-30: every candidate is measured; one that fails on any seed is rejected. The lowest mean wins.
- Report seeds 31-60 (held out): the winner and the runner-up, compared seed by seed.
- Skips: text True, cutscenes True; engine talkspeed 255; bridge speedrun-bridge v1.
