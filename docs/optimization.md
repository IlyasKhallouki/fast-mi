## Method

The objective is the expected number of ticks from the segment start to the goal, that is, the mean over
seeds. Fast Downward (`astar(lmcut())`) does all of the search. Each action costs its measured mean ticks,
rounded and at least 1, and walks are costed per link. This static cost table is only a *surrogate*,
because real durations depend on context. The optimiser therefore picks the winner by measurement and
reports it on held-out seeds.

- Surrogate: position-keyed means (see the section below).
- Earlier measurements: 47 summaries (825 runs) were pooled before the first iteration (bridge `speedrun-bridge v2`). Skipped: 21 summaries whose skips, engine pins or bridge differ, or whose plan the model no longer has. Runs on the report seeds are never pooled.
- Explore seeds 1-5 drive the optimistic loop. An unmeasured action costs 1, a lower bound; an unmeasured (action, position) pair costs the action's minimum, an optimistic guess. Each plan that contains unmeasured actions is measured and its samples are pooled. The loop stops when the plan has only measured actions and repeats.
- Candidates: the mean-cost plan, the unit-cost plan, the pooled-mean plan, 20 plans from bootstrap-sampled cost vectors (RNG seed 0) and 20 plans from the mean costs, each multiplied by a factor drawn from U[0.9, 1.1] (RNG seed 1). Plans with the same action sequence count once.
- Select seeds 1-30: every candidate is measured on these. A candidate that fails on any seed is rejected, and the lowest mean wins.
- Report seeds 31-60 are held out. Only the winner and the runner-up are measured on them, and the two are compared seed by seed.
- Skips: text True, cutscenes True; engine talkspeed 255; bridge speedrun-bridge v2.

## Position-keyed surrogate

A walk can take hundreds of ticks more or less depending on where the previous action left ego, while
the top candidates differ by much less. The keyed surrogate therefore charges each action its mean ticks
in the position ego starts it from. That position is a token that `speedrun.positions` derives from the
plan alone. It is `start` at the beginning of the plan; `entry:<from>:<to>:<id>` after a room change
through exit object `<id>`, or `entry:<from>:<to>` when no sentence makes the change (the helmet click,
for example); and `obj:<room>:<id>` after a sentence on a room object, where the object is the last
sentence step's (`obj2` first). Inventory-only, dialogue-only and click-only actions leave it unchanged.

- Mode: on, with key rooms `all`. Keyed rooms: `bar-left`, `bar-right`, `clearing`, `cu-dock`, `dock`, `f201`, `f202`, `f203`, `f204`, `f205`, `f206`, `f207a`, `f207b`, `f208`, `f209`, `f210`, `f211`, `f212`, `f213`, `f214`, `f215`, `f216`, `f217`, `f218`, `f219`, `f220`, `foyer`, `high-street-mansion`, `high-street-town`, `jail`, `kitchen`, `lookout`, `low-street`, `mansion`, `melee-map`, `store`, `tent`, `treasure-site`, `underwater` (103 position tokens in the model). All other rooms share the context `any`, and their actions cost their pooled mean.
- An (action, position) pair that was never measured costs the action's minimum measured duration. This is an optimistic guess, not a proven lower bound: it makes the planner try the pair, and the loop then measures it. An action that was never measured costs 1 tick.
